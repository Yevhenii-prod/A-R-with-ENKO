Worked example

Say an artist generates $10,000 in gross royalties this quarter. At 1M–2M listeners on a 70/30 deal, you earn 4.0% = $400 this quarter — about $1,600 a year. Build a roster of ten artists like this and that's ~$16,000/year, recurring for up to three years.
